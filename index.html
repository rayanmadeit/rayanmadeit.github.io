THE DREAM I NEVER HAD — MORAN MORADI

MUSIC & SOUND DESIGN BY RAYAN MADE IT
01 / 02

I’m a Paris-based composer and sound artist. After ten years of making music, my approach has become increasingly instinctive and reductive: looking for the simplest way to express what matters. Whether working from an image, a story or an artist, the process begins by looking for what makes it singular: its tension, emotion, rhythm, or sometimes something harder to name. Music becomes a way of revealing what is already there, rather than adding another layer to it. Every project has its own language. The work is to find it.
